# Samples of the PressMint-ES-CM corpus
The source of the PressMint-ES-CM corpus will be a selection of texts from the digital libraries Patrimonio Digital Complutense, Biblioteca Digital de Madrid and Hemeroteca Municipal de Madrid.
## Data source

The source of the PressMint-ES-CM corpus will be from the digital libraries Patrimonio Digital Complutense, Biblioteca Digital de Madrid and Hemeroteca Municipal de Madrid.

### Details of the source:

* __Source__: Patrimonio Digital Complutense 

* __Availability__: The texts are in Spanish (es) language and covers three magazines: Estampa(1928-1938), Civdad(1934-1935) and Aire Libre(1923-1925).

* __Content__: 

* __Size__:
  Size:
  ● Estampa
      ○ 68 issues (48 pages per issue) = 3264 pages.
  ● Civdad
      ○ 19 issues (32 pages per issue) = 608 pages.
  ● Aire Libre
      ○ 106 issues (30 pages per issue) = 3180 pages.
  

* __Structure__: It is possible to reconstruct all relations collection-issue-volume-copie-page. Pages corresponds to scan of the page in jpg format and automaticaly recognized text.
